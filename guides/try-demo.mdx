of read-only access. You can use the same [read-only setup](/guides/security) when
connecting your own exchange accounts.
</Note>

## Try it with your AI agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your AI agent">
    Follow the [Plugin or MCP Server Setup](/guides/mcp-server-setup) guide for your
    preferred client. It covers ChatGPT & Codex, Claude, Cursor, OpenClaw, Hermes,
    and other compatible MCP clients.

    Use the browser-based OAuth path. You do not need to create a Developer API key
    or add an exchange connection for this demo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize the demo account">
    When your client opens the Portfobit authorization page, sign in with the demo
    credentials above. If you are already signed in to your own Portfobit account,
    sign out before starting the demo authorization so you connect the demo account.

    Keep the default permission selection so you can explore accounts, portfolios,
    orders, activity history, and market data. No additional trading or
    internal-transfer permissions are needed for this demo. Approve the
    authorization and return to your AI client.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask about the real portfolio">
    Copy one of the prompts below into your AI conversation. The exchange accounts
    are already connected; start by reading their existing data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Check balances

```text
Use Portfobit to list the connected accounts and summarize their balances and total portfolio value. Include the latest synchronization time and flag any accounts whose data is unavailable or stale.
```

### Explore asset exposure

```text
Use Portfobit to show the largest asset exposures across the connected accounts. Compare BTC, ETH, and stablecoin holdings where available, and explain any concentration in the portfolio.
```

### Analyze open positions

```text
Use Portfobit to list any open positions and summarize their direction, size, unrealized P&L, and available risk indicators. If there are no open positions, say so. Use read-only queries and include the data timestamps.
```

## What to expect

- **Real accounts and funds.** You are querying actual exchange account data
  through Portfobit and MCP.
- **Changing data.** Balances, prices, and positions can change. Responses reflect
  the latest available synchronized data; ask for timestamps when comparing results.
  See [Sync and Data Freshness](/guides/sync-and-data-freshness).
- **A shared experience.** Everyone uses the same demo account. Use it to explore
  the existing connections and data; use your own Portfobit account for personal
  exchange connections and settings.
- **Read-only exchange access.** This demo demonstrates portfolio queries and
  analysis. Portfobit's protected trading features require separately enabled
  permissions, explicit user confirmation, and a trading OTP. See
  [Security](/guides/security) for the full requirements.
